# Chrome Web Store listing draft

**Name:** M9R Web Presence

**Short description (max 132 chars):** Watch and direct your AI agents on the pages you choose, with approvals. Needs the free M9R app on your computer.

**Single purpose (dashboard field):** Connect the user's browser pages to the M9R app on their own computer, so the AI agents they run there can work on those pages in view of the user and with the user's approval.

**Detailed description:**

M9R lets the AI agents you already run on your computer (Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode) work on real web pages together, in front of you.

Requires the free M9R app on your computer (install it with one command: `npx m9r-cli connect`). The extension does nothing on its own. Get the app and setup steps at https://m9r.dev.

What you get
- See each agent as a named, colored cursor on the page with a short note about what it is doing.
- A small message bar on the page: tap Alt+M to open it, or hold Alt+M and speak. Type "Claude, open the docs" or "all of you, check the pricing page" and it goes to those agents.
- A thread pill (Alt+N) with the conversation and any approvals waiting for you. Anything consequential (submitting, buying, posting, deleting) waits for your yes.
- Site access is yours to grant, one site at a time. A single click stops every agent action.

Privacy in short
- Your page data goes only to the M9R app on your own computer and to the agents you selected. The extension has no analytics or advertising.
- The microphone is used only while you hold Alt+M. Chrome's built-in speech recognition turns speech into text (it sends audio to Google's speech service); M9R never receives or keeps the audio.
- Full details: https://m9r.dev/privacy/extension

The extension is free. Optional paid plans for shared workspaces and history are available on m9r.dev.
