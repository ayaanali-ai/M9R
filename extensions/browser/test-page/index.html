Shipping

Orders ship from our warehouse within two business days. Standard delivery takes five to seven days inside the continental United States.

International orders are quoted at checkout and ship with tracking. Customs duties are the responsibility of the recipient.

Orders placed after 2pm Pacific are processed the following business day. We do not ship on weekends or holidays.

Returns

Unused items can be returned within thirty days for a refund to the original payment method. Return shipping is free for defective items.

Custom and personalized items cannot be returned unless they arrive damaged. Refunds are issued within five business days of receipt.

To start a return, include your order number and the reason for the return in the form below.
