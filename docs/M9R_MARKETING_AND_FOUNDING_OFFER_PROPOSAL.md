# M9R Marketing and Founding Offer Proposal

Status: proposal for review. No price, payment processor, or public claim is locked until the owner approves the final offer and the product evidence supports it.

## Positioning

M9R should be presented as the place where the agents people already use can work on their computer.

Core idea:

> Your agents already know how to think. M9R gives them a place to work.

The message is provider-neutral and experience-first. M9R does not replace a user's Codex, Claude, OpenCode, or other provider subscription. It gives those agents a shared desktop surface, coordination layer, controls, and a simple operating experience.

## Voice

- Calm, precise, confident, and slightly rebellious.
- One idea per post or video.
- Short human sentences.
- Let the product demonstration prove the claim.
- Avoid feature laundry lists, generic AI language, fake urgency, and unsupported superlatives.
- Do not use em-dash-heavy AI copy, vague phrases such as “seamless revolution,” or claims that have not been demonstrated.
- Every public claim must have a reproducible demo or a clearly labeled roadmap status.

## Founding offer

Proposed name: **Founding Lifetime License**.

The first 500 accepted customers receive a one-time founding price and lifetime access to the M9R desktop product and core updates under clearly published terms.

The offer must state plainly:

- The one-time fee covers M9R software access and core product updates.
- Provider subscriptions, provider usage fees, model charges, and third-party services remain the customer's responsibility.
- “Lifetime” means the lifetime of the M9R product or service, not the lifetime of a person.
- The supported-device limit, account transfer rules, refund window, and end-of-service policy must be visible before payment.
- No dark-pattern countdowns or undisclosed price changes.

Recommended conversion path:

1. Join the free waitlist.
2. See a real product demonstration.
3. Receive the founding offer with a clear refund policy.
4. Pay once to claim a numbered founding place.
5. Receive early access, founding status, and the same core product updates as the product evolves.

If referrals are used, reward genuine interest rather than spam. A referral should earn waitlist priority or a transparent credit only after the invited person confirms a real signup. It should never require public posting or misleading claims.

## Launch structure

### Main reveal

One 45–60 second faceless motion piece:

1. Agents live in separate places.
2. The user asks for one outcome.
3. M9R brings the agents into one desktop workspace.
4. The agents act, coordinate, and report back.
5. The user sees the result, not a configuration tutorial.
6. End with the founding invitation.

Use a real Open Screens product capture as proof. Motion graphics should frame the story, not disguise missing product behavior.

### Weekly feature stories

Release one focused story at a time:

- Agents working where the user works.
- Provider-neutral model choice.
- Desktop actions without taking over the owner's cursor.
- Multiple agents sharing work without colliding.
- Dictation that feels immediate.
- Persistent agent identity and cursor colors.
- Safe credentials and permissions.
- One-click installation and controlled updates.

Each story gets one short video, one proof post, one technical follow-up for people who want details, and one waitlist link.

## Landing page structure

1. One-sentence promise.
2. Ten-second product clip.
3. Three concrete things M9R lets agents do.
4. Provider-neutral explanation.
5. Privacy and control explanation.
6. Founding offer with exact inclusions and exclusions.
7. FAQ covering provider subscriptions, installation, updates, refunds, and device limits.
8. Waitlist or purchase action.

These are drafts. They must be checked against the actual accepted build before publication.

## Proof and honesty rules

- Do not claim a capability until it passes a repeatable acceptance task.
- Label experimental provider integrations as experimental.
- Do not claim universal anti-bot behavior.
- Do not imply M9R supplies a provider's model access or subscription.
- Keep credentials, cookies, tokens, and private task data out of marketing captures.
- Publish a short “what is live today” list beside every major launch.
