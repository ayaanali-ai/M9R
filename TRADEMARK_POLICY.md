# M9R trademark policy

Updated 2026-09-10. This policy is part of the release boundary; it is not a
trademark registration or legal opinion.

## Reserved marks

M9R reserves the names, logos, icons, domains, and product identifiers used to
identify the M9R project and its hosted services, including:

- `M9R`;
- the M9R star/mark artwork and branded product graphics;
- `M9R Cloud` and other M9R hosted-service names;
- `OathLock` and `oathlock` as historical/compatibility identifiers.

The repository code license does not grant trademark rights. The `oathlock`
CLI name and `.oathlock` directory remain load-bearing compatibility
identifiers; their presence in a fork does not authorize the fork to market
itself as the official M9R or OathLock product.

## Allowed uses

Without separate written permission, a project may:

- state that it is “built with M9R” or “compatible with M9R” when that is true;
- link to the official M9R project while accurately describing the relationship;
- reproduce unmodified license and notice text;
- use the names `M9R` or `OathLock` only as necessary to accurately describe
  the original project, compatibility, or an unmodified dependency.

These uses must not imply endorsement, sponsorship, certification, employment,
or an official distribution relationship.

## Not allowed without permission

Do not:

- name a fork, hosted service, company, domain, package, or app `M9R`,
  `OathLock`, or a confusingly similar name;
- use the M9R logo, star mark, product screenshots, or branded UI as the
  primary identity of another product;
- imply that a fork is the official M9R Cloud, an M9R-certified provider, or a
  supported M9R deployment;
- register a domain, package, social account, or company name that incorporates
  an M9R reserved mark;
- remove or alter attribution in a way that suggests M9R endorses the fork.

Forks should choose their own product identity and use wording such as
“a community fork based on M9R” where accurate.

## Contributions and branding

Contributors retain ownership of their own names and marks. A contribution to
the codebase does not transfer trademark rights or authorize use of a
contributor's identity. M9R may publish compatible branding assets separately
from the code license.

## Reporting confusion

Report suspected impersonation or confusing use of M9R marks through the
security contact in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md). Trademark reports are not
security vulnerabilities, so include the public URL, screenshots, and a short
description of the confusion rather than private credentials or exploit data.

This policy should be reviewed by counsel before the first public release and
before registering or enforcing any mark.
